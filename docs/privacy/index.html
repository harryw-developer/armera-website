Privacy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

The short version

  • This website does not use cookies.
  • It carries no advertising and no social-media tracking.
  • We do not sell your information, or pass it to anyone for their marketing.
  • We only hold personal details about you if you register a guarantee or get in touch with us.

Who we are

This website is run by ARMERA Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 12405257). Our registered office is Units 1 & 2 Northlands Industrial Estate, Copheap Lane, Warminster, Wiltshire BA12 0BG, UK. For the purposes of UK data protection law we are the “controller” of the personal information described on this page, which means we decide how it is used and are responsible for it.

If you have any question about your information, or want to use any of the rights set out below, contact us at info@armera.co.uk, on 01225 251204, or by post at the address above.

What we collect, why, and for how long

When you register a guarantee

What: your name, email address and postal address; where and when you bought the product; the products you are registering; and, if you choose to give them, the name of your developer, the date you moved in, your installer’s name and address, and a photograph or copy of your proof of purchase.

Why: to register your guarantee, to deal with any claim you make under it, and to contact you if there is something you need to know about a product you own, such as a safety notice.

Our legal basis: it is necessary for the guarantee, which is a contract between us and you. We also have a legitimate interest in keeping accurate records of the products we have guaranteed.

How long: for as long as the guarantee lasts — up to 25 years, depending on the product — and for up to six years after it ends, which is the period in which a legal claim could be brought.

If you give us your installer’s details, please make sure they are happy for you to do so. We use them only in connection with your guarantee.

When you order a colour swatch

What: your name, postal address and email address, the colours you asked for and, if you choose to give them, your company, telephone number and any note you add.

Why: to post the samples to you and to contact you about your request.

Our legal basis: it is necessary to do what you have asked us to do.

How long: normally for no more than two years after your request.

When you contact us

What: your contact details and whatever you tell us, whether by email, telephone or post.

Why: to answer your enquiry and to help with spares, advice or a problem with a product.

Our legal basis: our legitimate interest in responding to people who get in touch with us.

How long: for as long as it takes to deal with your enquiry, and normally for no more than two years afterwards unless it relates to a guarantee.

When you visit this website

What: which pages are viewed, the kind of device used (phone, tablet or computer), the website that led to ours, and a random number that lets us count pages viewed in one visit. We do not record your IP address, your name, or anything else that identifies you, and the random number is forgotten as soon as you close the tab.

Why: to understand which pages and products are useful, so we can improve the website.

Our legal basis: our legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used. You can object at any time using the button below.

How long: no longer than 14 months.

The website also respects your browser’s “Do Not Track” setting: if it is switched on, your visits are never counted.

When you search for a retailer

The town or postcode you type is sent to a postcode lookup service so the map can show the right area. We do not keep it.

Keeping the website secure

Like every website, ours is sent your device’s IP address in order to deliver pages to it. Our hosting provider uses it briefly to do that and to block abuse — for example, to stop a form being submitted thousands of times. We do not keep a record of it.

Cookies and your browser’s storage

This website sets no cookies. It uses your browser’s own storage in two small ways: the random visit number described above, which lasts only until you close the tab; and, if you choose “Don’t count my visits”, a note of that choice so we can honour it next time. Neither is shared with anyone.

Who we share your information with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the companies that provide services we need to run this website, and only as far as they need it:

  • Cloudflare, which hosts the website and securely stores its database and files, acting on our instructions.
  • CARTO and OpenStreetMap, which supply the map on the “Find a retailer” page. Your browser fetches the map images directly from them, so they see its IP address.
  • postcodes.io, which looks up the town or postcode you search for.

We may also disclose information where the law requires us to.

Where your information is held

Some of the companies above operate internationally, so information may be processed outside the United Kingdom. Where that happens we rely on UK adequacy regulations, or on safeguards approved under UK data protection law such as the International Data Transfer Agreement.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is wrong or incomplete;
  • ask us to delete your information, where we no longer need it;
  • ask us to restrict how we use it;
  • object to our using it — including for the visit statistics described above;
  • ask for the information you gave us in a form you can take elsewhere.

To use any of these, contact us using the details above. There is no charge, and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy

Please tell us first, so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office, the UK’s data protection regulator: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, telephone 0303 123 1113, or by post to Wycliffe House, Water Lane, Wilmslow, Cheshire SK9 5AF.

A few other things

You do not have to give us any personal information to use this website. If you register a guarantee, the details marked as required are needed to register it; without them we cannot.

No automated decisions. We do not use your information to make decisions about you by automated means, and we do not build profiles of visitors.

Children. This website is not aimed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Links to other websites. Links to our social media pages, to videos and to map directions open on those companies’ own websites, which have their own privacy policies. Nothing is sent to them unless you follow the link.

Changes to this page. If we change how we use your information we will update this page and change the date at the top.
